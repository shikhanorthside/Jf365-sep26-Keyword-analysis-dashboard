Strong returns
Blended ROAS of 7.12x — ₹6,02,430 in ad sales on ₹84,600, at an average cost per click of ₹22.09. Brand (15.6x), Exact (9.1x) and Product Keywords (8.2x) campaigns all returned above 8x.
Sponsored Brands is the standout
SB · Jackfruit Flour returned ₹97,301 on just ₹3,404 (28.6x) — 16% of ad sales from 4% of spend. Brand-keyword targets delivered 18.9x.
Category leadership
On "jackfruit flour" Jackfruit365 holds 71.7% of purchases and 58.0% of clicks, with impression rank 2.46. It holds rank 1.0 on "jackfruit powder" and on its own brand terms (~96% share).
"Powder" is the biggest search term
"jackfruit powder" drew 5,444 searches in September versus 4,046 for "jackfruit flour". Jackfruit365's click share on "powder" is 44% against 58% on "flour" — the largest room to grow.
